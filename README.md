# Halcira

A privacy-first email app that respects your time. For Windows 11, with Lisa, a built-in email assistant.

**Download:** grab `Halcira-Setup-x.y.z.exe` from the latest release under [Releases](../../releases/latest) and run it.
Windows SmartScreen may warn that the installer isn't signed. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Once installed, Halcira updates itself: new versions download in the background and install the next time you quit,
or right away from **Settings → General → Updates**.

**Halcira for Android:** download `Halcira-Android-x.y.z.apk` from the same release on your phone and open it
(allow installing from your browser when Android asks). Then on your PC open **Settings → Phone → Link a phone**
and scan the code. Your phone gets your mail, Lisa and your settings through your PC, end-to-end encrypted.

This repository holds release builds only.

## Your privacy

Halcira runs on your PC. Your mail, passwords and Lisa's notes stay there, encrypted with your passcode.
There's no Halcira server, no analytics and no ads.
